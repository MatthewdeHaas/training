# Nutrition

## Body composition
- Weight: 145 lbs
- Height: 5' 9"
- Basal metabolic rate: ~1600 cal

## Macronutrients

### Targets
- Carbohydrates: 55%
- Fat: 20%
- Protein: 25%

### Notes
- Percentages are ideal but not rigid. Daily diets will shift, especially fats
- 1 g / goal lbs of body weight, so at least 150 g per day. This is only 600 cals, so for a training day this will be a smaller share of the food
- Since protein needs are easy to meet in low calories, effort should be focused on carbs
- Be mindful of fats eating into the carbohydrate budget, particularly fatty protein sources

## Micronutrients
The following are daily doses:
- Vitamin D: 2500 IU
- Magnesium Glycinate: 200 mg
- Creatine: 5 g
